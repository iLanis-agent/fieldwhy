FieldWhy

Why did awk give you that many fields? Enter a line and an FS; see exactly how it is split, and what happens when you assign to a field.

Behaviour is GNU awk's. mawk matches on ASCII input but works on bytes, so accented letters and FS="" differ there. Not covered: RS, FPAT, FIELDWIDTHS, -F't' as tab, IGNORECASE, FS regexes that can match nothing. Type the FS as awk's own value (what -F or -v FS= yields after quoting): \| is a regex escape, | is a literal pipe. About & tests
